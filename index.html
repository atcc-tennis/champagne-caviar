The reason we're raising a glass

Every point, on the record.

Proceeds bring PlaySight SmartCourt cameras to ATCC — the AI video technology trusted by tour pros, top colleges, and academies — capturing every match and rally so members and pros can review play, sharpen technique, and train smarter. One unforgettable night; a lasting upgrade for everyone who steps on court.

What your night funds

  • PlaySight SmartCourt cameras that record play, automatically
  • Instant match & rally replay for members
  • Film-review support for lessons and clinics
  • A training edge for juniors, adults, and teams alike

Why it matters

The best players in the world study the tape. With cameras on our courts, every ATCC member gets that same advantage — see your game the way your pro sees it, and watch your progress point by point. Your ticket and your hosting turn a great party into a permanent home-court upgrade.
