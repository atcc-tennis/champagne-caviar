Installing PlaySight SmartCourt cameras on our courts is an investment in player development — giving every ATCC member a training tool that has long been standard for high-level athletes. By bringing this technology to the club, we create more opportunities to learn, improve, and enjoy the game.

Why Court Cameras Matter

Tennis players at every level — from juniors to professionals — use video analysis to improve. Watching yourself play provides insights that are nearly impossible to recognize in the moment.

See what is really happening

Many of us think we know what we're doing on court, but video often tells a different story. It reveals movement, court positioning, shot selection, footwork, recovery, and patterns that are easy to miss while playing.

Learn faster

Whether you're working on a stronger serve, better net play, or improving doubles strategy, reviewing video helps reinforce good habits and identify opportunities for improvement. A few minutes of review can make the next lesson or practice much more productive.

Get more value from coaching

Pros can review clips with players after matches or clinics to provide specific, personalized feedback. Instead of relying on memory, players and coaches can see exactly what happened and discuss how to improve.

Continue improving off the court

Members often talk about putting in work away from the court. Video review is one of the most effective ways to do that — you can learn from your own matches anytime, not just during a lesson.

Support every level of player

Court cameras benefit juniors developing fundamentals, league players preparing for competition, and recreational players who simply want to improve and have more fun.
