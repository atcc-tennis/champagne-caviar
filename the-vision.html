Installing PlaySight cameras on our courts is an investment in our tennis program and player development. The cameras and AI analytics give every member — at any level — the free option to use this technology to learn, improve, and enjoy the game, and to review their tennis with their coaches.

Who is PlaySight?

Founded in 2010, PlaySight was developed specifically for tennis and other racquet sports. It is used by more than 100 NCAA Division I collegiate tennis programs, as well as the USTA National Campus, IMG Academy, and premier private clubs such as the La Jolla Beach and Tennis Club.

Why use PlaySight?

PlaySight is designed to enhance the tennis experience for members of every age and skill level — whether taking lessons, participating in clinics, playing social doubles, representing the club in USTA league competition, or competing in tournaments.

Lessons, practice sessions and matches can be selected for recording, letting players review stroke mechanics, strategy, shot selection and court positioning after play. Coaches can review video with players, create teaching clips, and provide more objective instruction — while USTA teams can use match footage to evaluate doubles positioning, prepare for upcoming opponents, and review match strategy. Players have secure access to their recordings through the PlaySight app.
