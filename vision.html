Why We're Raising a Glass
The Vision

One unforgettable night — to put a camera on every court, for every player, for years to come.

Every dollar raised at Champagne & Caviar goes to bringing PlaySight smart-camera systems to the courts at Altadena Town & Country Club — turning every court into one that sees, records, and remembers.

What Cameras on the Courts Unlock

Every point, played back

No more "you had to be there." Members can record a match, review it instantly, and share the highlights — from a junior's first tournament win to a hard-fought league final.

Better tennis, faster

Film is how players improve. Our juniors, adults, and pros gain a coaching tool that used to belong only to the tour — see your game, study it, grow it.

Family courtside, from anywhere

Parents, grandparents, and friends who can't make it to the club can watch live — so no one misses the moment that matters.

A club that celebrates itself

Stream club tournaments and events, build a shared highlight reel of ATCC life, and bring our tennis community closer, on and off the court.

It's a lasting upgrade that will serve every member, for years — funded by one great evening together. Come be part of it.
